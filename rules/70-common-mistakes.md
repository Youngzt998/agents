# Common mistakes

Each entry is a mistake that has occurred, recorded on the author's order and
grouped by the kind of work it belongs to. The author's own words close each
entry, with an English rendering after them.

## Benchmark / evaluation

**Control the variables of a comparison.** When benchmark results are compared,
every factor that is not the purpose of the comparison is the same on all sides.
Examples of such factors: the version of the toolchain that produced each
compared artifact, and algorithms unrelated to the one under comparison.
Author (2026-11-16): "benchmark 对比的时候要注意控制变量 - 比如注意所有不是对比目的
因子（比如生产工具链的版本、无关算法）是否相同" (English: "when benchmarks are
compared, take care to control the variables: for example check that every
factor that is not the purpose of the comparison, such as the version of the
production toolchain or an unrelated algorithm, is the same").
